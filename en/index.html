About me

My name is Ing. Erin Zeleňáková and I operate Erinka Taxi.

Erinka Taxi was originally created to provide safe rides for women.
Over time, it has expanded to include family rides, senior transport, transport for children to school, airport transfers, celebrations ...

People are different. Some enjoy talking during the ride, while others prefer to relax in silence. I respect both.

My goal is to make sure you reach your destination safely, comfortably and with the feeling that you were in good hands.

You may also know me from Bolt, Uber or ZoberMa,
where you can also meet me as a driver outside of pre-booked rides.

I look forward to driving you.
